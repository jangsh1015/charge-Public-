About call interruption due to no response

When communicating with the other side and it does not respond to your call for various reasons, FT8CN will repeat the call with the message content of the current stage. A call will not proceed to the next stage until the other side responds. After setting no reply counter, when the number of repeated calls reaches the set value, automatic program will stop communicating to the target and move on to the next target.

Since 0.93-kr3, a station abandoned this way for no reply is excluded from automatic re-calls for 30 minutes. Even if the same station calls CQ again, auto-follow skips it for 30 minutes and looks for another new CQ station. Tapping the callsign yourself, or the station calling you first, still works. The counter can be set from Ignore (0) to 30 calls.

Since 0.93-kr7 the RR73 stage also stops at the same count. Previously that stage alone waited twice as long before giving up.
